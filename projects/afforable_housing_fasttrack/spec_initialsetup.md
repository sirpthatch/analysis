**Objective**

This is a project directory to reverse-engineer, before the City Planning
Commission publishes it, the list of twelve NYC community districts that will
be placed on the affordable-housing "fast track" created by the November 2025
charter amendment (Question 2). The list is due no later than October 1, 2026,
and covers the five-year cycle that closed June 30, 2026.

The project should a) collect the data the adopted CPC methodology depends on
(HPD affordable housing production, DOB construction permits, and DCP housing
stock, all from NYC Open Data), b) profile it to understand where it is
incomplete or mismatched against the rule, c) apply the rule as written to
produce a projected list with an honest account of its uncertainty, d) compare
that rate-based list against the count-based list most coverage assumes, and
e) produce a writeup for an NYC urban-development Substack.

**Research Questions**

1. Which twelve community districts does the adopted methodology actually
   select, run against the completed first cycle?
2. How far does the rate-based ranking the rule specifies diverge from the
   count-based ranking most coverage assumes — and specifically, does it pull
   in large, wealthy, high-stock Manhattan districts that built *more*
   affordable units in absolute terms than the outer-borough districts they
   displace?
3. Is the stock-blindness of the denominator a choice voters made in the
   ballot language, or a choice DCP made in rulemaking? Commenters asked for
   existing affordable stock to be credited and were refused — on what stated
   grounds?
4. How stable is the projected list? Which districts sit close enough to the
   twelfth position that data gaps, the missing final quarter, or a different
   reading of the rule would flip them?

**Research Method**

1. Collect data
2. Profile and analyze it
3. Explore questions
4. Document limitations
5. Repeat 3 & 4 until satisfactory findings are made (with human input)
6. Compare against DCP's official list once published, and write up any
   divergence
7. Publish results
