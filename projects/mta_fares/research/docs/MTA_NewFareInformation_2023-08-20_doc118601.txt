How to use OMNY
OMNY is the MT A ’s contactless fare payment system. OMNY readers are 
available at every subway entrance and on every bus. Choose the payment 
method that works best for you.
• T ap your own smartphone or device with a mobile wallet.
• T ap your own contactless credit, debit, prepaid, or pre-tax card.
• T ap an OMNY card, available to buy and fill at stores near you.
OMNY fare cap
OMNY caps your weekly fares at $34 when you tap the same card or device for 
every ride. This gives OMNY customers the benefits of a 7-Day Unlimited MetroCard 
without paying in advance.
Y our first tap starts a new seven-day cap. If you spend $34 within seven days,  
you ride free for the rest of the cap period. If you spend less, you only pay for  
the rides you take.
The cap resets every seven days and restarts with your next tap. Express buses, 
group trips, and transfers do not count toward the cap. The Reduced-Fare cap is $17.
