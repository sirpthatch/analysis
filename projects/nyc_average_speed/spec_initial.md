**Objective**

This is a project directory to explore how the traffic speed in midtown manhattan changes over the course of the year and in response to special events such as the UNGA week.  It should be set up to a) collect data necessary for the analysis (particularly from NYC opendata from the TLC trip data), b) Perform exploratory analysis to understand the shape and features of the data, c) Perform more detailed analysis on the research questions outlined below, and d) Create a final analysis writeup that can be posted on a nyc urban developement themed substack.

**Research Questions**

1. Over the course of a standard year, how does the average speed of surface streets change?
2. What is the impact of UNGA?  Is it in fact the slowest week of the year, and if not what is?
3. How has the introduction of congestion prices affected the traffic in midtown?  The expectation is that trips would be faster and there would be fewer rides.  Is that the case?

**Research Method**

1. Collect data
2. Profile and analyze it
3. Explore questions
4. Document limitations
5. Repeat 3 & 4 until satisfactory findings are made (with human input)
6. Publish results
