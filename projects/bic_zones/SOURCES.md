# Sources

## The news hook

**City Exempted Truck in Fatal Crash from Life-Saving Safety Component** — Streetsblog NYC,
2026-09-21. https://nyc.streetsblog.org/2026/09/21/city-exempted-truck-in-fatal-crash-from-life-saving-safety-component

Establishes: Daria Serkov, 15, killed on East 14th Street in Manhattan on 2026-09-18 by a Badger
hydro-excavation truck. BIC granted that vehicle a side-guard exemption in 2023. BIC confirmed
approximately 312 waivers issued this year and 572 the previous year, and that about half of all
trade waste trucks in the city have been exempted. Side guards mandated since 2015 with a 2023
compliance deadline. BIC's stated justification: vehicle features such as toolboxes, ladders and
fuel tanks on the sidewalls render guards impractical (Leah Nomkin, BIC Director of Policy).
Article cites studies showing underguards reduce fatalities by 40 percent.

*This is the single most important source in the bundle. The 312 / 572 waiver figures exist
nowhere in Open Data — they come from BIC's statement to Streetsblog. See the FOIL note in
NOTES.md.*
